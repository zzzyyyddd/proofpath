Devpost Learn · 02 / Scope review

ProofPath

Understand what changed in hackathon rules—and inspect the evidence before deciding what to do.

Approved · 2-scope completeCanonical scope: Markdown

Planning companion only. No application implementation or PRD has begun.

The person & problem

An independent developer preparing an international hackathon submission has an old and a new rules document.

Manual comparison takes time; confident AI summaries can obscure weak evidence. Missing a changed requirement could affect the submission.

The distinctive idea

Connect each detected change to exact source passages, then separate that document evidence from AI interpretation of its possible impact.

The developer inspects the evidence and decides what to review. ProofPath does not decide eligibility.

One complete workflow

Return when another rules update needs review.

  1. Upload & validate
    Exactly two English documents labeled Old Version and New Version.
  2. Compare actual text
    Extract and analyze uploaded content, without predefined findings.
  3. Review the summary
    Inspect Added, Removed, and Modified findings.
  4. Inspect the proof
    Open exact, source-labeled quotations; validate each against extracted text.
  5. Understand implications
    Separate AI impact interpretation, suggested actions, and uncertainty.
  6. Make a human decision
    Check the actual submission and consult the official rules.

Scope boundaries

Expand each boundary to inspect the tradeoffs.

Now · the proof of concept
  • Plain text (.txt), Markdown (.md), and directly extractable text PDF (.pdf).
  • Exactly two English-language documents; maximum 2 MB and 30,000 extracted-text characters each, and 10 pages per PDF. Reject over-limit text with a clear explanation; never silently truncate.
  • Input validation, text extraction, requirement comparison, concise summary, and categorized findings.
  • Exact passages with programmatic quotation validation and links to their source document and reliable text location. TXT/Markdown use text offsets or line references; PDFs use reliable extracted-text locations, with page references only when extraction establishes them reliably. Separate AI impact analysis, review actions, and uncertainty.
  • Distinguish newer replacements from unresolved conflicts.
  • Clear handling of invalid files, extraction failures, and incomplete analysis. Findings with unverifiable quotations must never appear as verified evidence. Old/New labels establish neither authenticity nor official authority.
  • One polished, professional upload-to-evidence workflow.
Later · useful extensions outside this build

OCR; image, Word, and spreadsheet support; more than two documents; multilingual analysis; accounts; cloud storage and collaboration; historical version tracking; comprehensive exports; live web retrieval; blockchain verification.

These add ingestion, infrastructure, or workflow breadth without being necessary to prove the core comparison.

Cut · claims and workflows we will not promise
  • Eligibility, compliance, or disqualification decisions: the project has not been examined, and the app is not an official authority.
  • Complex legal or regulatory interpretation: beyond this focused use case.
  • Exhaustive detection or universal accuracy: controlled fixture success cannot establish either.
  • Generic chatbot and parallel workflows: preserve one complete journey.

Reliability boundaries

What can be checked directly

A displayed passage occurs in the corresponding extracted text and links to its source document and a reliable text location. Old and New evidence is clearly labeled. Known fixture changes are detected without flagging unchanged requirements.

What that does not establish

That a classification or interpretation is correct; that every change was found; that extracted text preserves every PDF relationship; or that supplied rules are authentic and current.

Findings with unverifiable quotations must never be presented as verified evidence. Surface failure or incomplete analysis when a reliable result cannot be supported.

Open decisions & technical risks

These are unresolved planning questions—not approved implementation choices. Product behavior belongs in 3-prd; technical decisions belong in 4-spec, after scope approval.

Evidence on the absent side · unresolved behavior

Additions and removals may have no corresponding passage in one document. A failed match does not prove absence. Do not fabricate quotations from the absent side. Define the evidence needed and safe handling of whitespace or formatting differences. Source-document links and reliable locations are required; page references depend on reliable PDF extraction.

Semantic correctness · classification risk

Real quotations can still support a mistaken interpretation. Define how replacement, unresolved conflict, and ambiguity are distinguished and presented.

Input limits & PDF extraction · feasibility risk

The limit is 30,000 extracted-text characters per document in addition to 2 MB and 10 PDF pages. Reject over-limit documents with a clear explanation and never silently truncate. Define the precise character-counting convention in the technical spec. PDF tables, reading order, and partially scanned pages can lose meaning even when extraction returns text. Decide what is rejected or marked incomplete.

Failed validation & incomplete analysis · unresolved behavior

The PRD and spec must decide whether findings with unverifiable quotations are withheld or the comparison is marked incomplete, and how that outcome is labeled. Such findings must never appear as verified evidence.

Validation coverage · unproven assumption

The main demo covers one addition and two modifications. Additional controlled tests require zero detected changes for identical requirements and correct removal detection without invented absent-side quotations. Define detailed checks for conflicts, extraction failures, input limits, location traceability, all supported formats, and repeatability.

Source authority · supplied, not authenticated

Old and New are user-assigned labels and establish neither authenticity nor official authority. Publication dates, official provenance, and current rules are not independently verified; live retrieval is deferred.

Effort & timing · protect the kernel

Three formats plus semantic analysis may exceed the curriculum's 2–4 hours of active work. The learner is willing to invest more time, but format support should be reduced through explicit scope revision if necessary to protect evidence integrity.

Identity, motivation & delivery

Personal stake: make AI-assisted research more transparent for real hackathon and technical decisions while practicing Devpost's formal plan-first workflow.
